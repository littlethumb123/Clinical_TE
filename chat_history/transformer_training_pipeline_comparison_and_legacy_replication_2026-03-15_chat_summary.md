### The Legacy Double-Update Bug

The original `min_transformer_train.py` has a problematic training step:

```160:189:data_ingestion/Legacy/Train/python/min_transformer_train.py
        loss.backward()
        optimizer.step()
        
        torch.nn.utils.clip_grad_norm_(model.parameters(), 0.25)
        for p in model.parameters():
            p.data.add_(p.grad, alpha=-optimizer.param_groups[0]['lr'])
```

This applies **three** weight updates per batch:
1. `optimizer.step()` -- SGD update (momentum-corrected)
2. `clip_grad_norm_` -- clips gradients (after they were already applied!)
3. `p.data.add_(p.grad, alpha=-lr)` -- a second raw SGD update

This double update effectively makes the learning rate ~2-3x higher than configured. If the legacy model worked well despite this, the actual effective dynamics were unique to this implementation.
